# Crowne Plaza Madinah — Gate Route Review

Google Maps pedestrian directions from **Crowne Plaza Madinah** to **Al Salam Gate B** resolved to the named **باب السلام / Al Salam Gate** and returned **350 m / 5 minutes**.

A comparison from the same hotel origin to **Bab Al Baqi** resolved to the named **باب البقيع / Bab Al Baqi** at **450 m / 6 minutes**. The named Al Salam Gate destination is therefore the shorter reviewed walking option for publication.
