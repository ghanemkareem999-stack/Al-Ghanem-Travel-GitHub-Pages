# Coral Al Madinah Map Research Note — 18 August 2026

Google Maps was reviewed on 18 August 2026 for Coral Al Madinah Hotel. The result resolved **Coral Al Madinah Parking**, not a hotel property point. It must not be published as the hotel location or used to calculate an Al-Masjid an-Nabawi walking route.

The maps result contained user-supplied commentary claiming a historic name change. This is not authoritative evidence and is excluded. The Coral planning record remains location-pending until a trustworthy current operating identity and a dedicated hotel pin can be independently verified.
