# InterContinental Dar Al Iman Madinah — Gate Route Review

Google Maps pedestrian directions from **InterContinental Dar Al Iman Madinah, Badaah** to **Al Salam Gate B** resolved to **باب السلام / Al Salam Gate** at **1.1 km / 14 minutes**.

A comparison from the same hotel origin to the exact named Maps destination **Haram Courtyard Gate No 316 Side Starting**, at **FJ85+X47**, returned a shorter route of **650 m / 9 minutes** via a pedestrian path. The public profile may therefore use the literal numbered courtyard-gate name and must not call it Al Salam Gate.

The public map keeps distance and duration live through Google Maps rather than publishing these values as fixed copy.

Source: Google Maps walking-direction comparisons, accessed 21 August 2026.
