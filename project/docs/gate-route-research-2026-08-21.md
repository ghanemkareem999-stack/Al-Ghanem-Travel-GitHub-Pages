# Madinah Gate Route Research — 21 August 2026

## Dar Al Taqwa Hotel

Google Maps was queried in pedestrian mode from **Dar Al Taqwa Hotel** to **Al Salam Gate**. It resolved the destination label to **“Haram Courtyard Gate No 316 Side Starting”** at plus code **FJ85+X47**, and showed a route of **800 m / 10 minutes** via pedestrian paths.

The Saudi Press Agency describes Al Salam Gate as being on the southern side of Al-Masjid an-Nabawi. The available mapping result does not independently demonstrate that Gate 316 is the named Al Salam Gate. Therefore, the Dar Al Taqwa record remains **property-location only** until a source or map result confirms the relationship. No Al Salam Gate claim, distance, or duration has been published from this observation.
