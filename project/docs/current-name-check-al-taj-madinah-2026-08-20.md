# Al Taj Madinah — identity review outcome

**Review date:** 20 August 2026  
**Outcome:** No public replacement or location data added to the generic `al-taj-madinah` planning record.

| Source | Finding | Decision impact |
|---|---|---|
| Planning record | Uses the non-exact name **Al Taj Madinah**, with an unreviewed central-east position and gate narrative. | Cannot be treated as a current operating identity. |

## Publication decision

Keep `al-taj-madinah` visible only in its existing verification-pending state, with no map, images, route, gate, distance, facilities, ratings, or direct contact. Do not substitute Manarat Al Taj for the planning record without independent evidence that they are the same property. Serviced-apartment search results remain excluded from the public hotel directory.
