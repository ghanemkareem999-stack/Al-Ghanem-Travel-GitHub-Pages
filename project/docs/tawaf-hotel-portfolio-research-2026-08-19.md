## Access constraints encountered

The public Facebook group that surfaced the names **أبراج طابة** and **أبراج طابة الفضي** redirected to login. A direct Booking.com page for Tabah Towers did not render in the browser environment. These access limits do not invalidate the candidates, but they prevent treating the extracted names, address, or operational relationship as fully verified. The next evidence pass will use independently accessible location and operating sources.

The public Facebook page titled **فنادق أبراج طابة** is accessible as a product/service page, but the visible public view does not supply a named portfolio. Its photos do not have usable property descriptions, and a page-text search for a portfolio list returned no public match. The page therefore supports discovery only and is not a source for assigning media or other hotel facts.
