# Hotel Media Visual Audit

## Authorization basis

On 18 August 2026, the project owner confirmed that the supplied hotel-media archives were authorized for Al Ghanem Travel’s marketing use. Images are still subjected to a visual identity check before being associated with a named hotel profile.

Other mapped groups in contact sheets 3–4 show hotel buildings, interiors, or identifiable brand/property context consistent with their owner-supplied folder mapping. The portfolio uses these as gallery imagery only and does not infer amenities, routes, or distance claims from them.

Contact sheet 5 completed the audit. The clean Al Awali room image and the Al Firdous facade can remain under the owner-authorized folder mapping; no additional generic landmark imagery was found in that final set.

## Publication rule

Where an image is removed from a named hotel gallery, it remains outside the public site rather than being repurposed as a hotel-specific photograph. The catalogue continues to avoid unverified route, amenity, room, and location claims regardless of whether a gallery is present.
