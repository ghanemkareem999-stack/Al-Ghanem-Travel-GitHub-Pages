# Madinah Hotel Directory: Publication Policy

The imported 2026 Madinah planning list is treated as a **planning source**, not as verified live inventory. Every property can be shown with its supplied name, location category, distance range, walking or vehicle estimate, and the planning note. The directory must identify such records as source-review items and must not imply that a room, amenity, rating, exact property pin, official image, partnership, shuttle, price, or availability has been verified.

| Content type | Public treatment | Required evidence before publication as verified |
|---|---|---|
| Location zone and planning distance | Clearly labelled planning estimate | Official property location and route review |
| Hotel grade and classification | Carried as a planning classification | Current official or partner confirmation |
| Room configurations and amenities | Hidden until confirmed | Official property or partner-supplied material |
| Gallery images | Hidden unless rights are approved | Partner authorization or commercial licence, recorded with the media item |
| Map and walking route | Hidden without an exact reviewed hotel point and a named practical gate | Corrected Google Maps property point, nearest-gate point, pedestrian route, and review date |
| Customer reviews and ratings | Not published by this website at launch | Genuine, attributable, permissioned review workflow |

Internal editorial notes support the team’s source-review process. They are not customer reviews, they are not published to visitors, and they cannot be used as implied endorsements.
