## Nearest-gate routing control

On 17 August 2026, Google Maps searches for **Bab Uthman ibn Affan**, **Bab Bilal**, and **Bab Ali ibn Abi Talib** resolved to map places described as squares in the Haram precinct rather than clearly documented entrance points. Those search results are useful for research but are not enough to assert that they are the nearest practical entry gate from a particular hotel. The directory will therefore withhold gate-specific walking claims until a named gate point and pedestrian approach have been manually reviewed against the property’s actual street-side access.

On 18 August 2026, the Pullman’s own official site was reviewed and expressly identified **Al Salam Gate** as just steps from the hotel. The directory therefore publishes Al Salam Gate as the map destination for Pullman only. Dar Al Iman and Anwar Al Madinah Mövenpick remain in the gate-pending state because their first-party pages corroborate location context but do not yet identify a specific practical gate for the mapped pedestrian approach.
